About

A little orientation.

I study philosophy and mathematics at Penn, where my interests include mathematical logic, finite model theory, and the philosophy of mathematics. I have also worked in paleobotany and planetary-analog field research, and I work in funeral service in the Philadelphia area.

This site is meant to be neither a résumé in disguise nor a personal feed: it is a place to make research and thought available in a form that can remain useful.

Attribution

On the shape and colors of this site.

This site takes its calm, section-forward architecture from the academic personal sites of Aaron Anderson and Hermann W. Pfefferkorn, and the first version of Andrew’s own site. Pfefferkorn’s On Science page suggested making the material choices visible rather than treating them as background.

The palette borrows the idea of colors with a story: blue-gray for slate and water; iron red for brick, clay, and Philadelphia; olive gray for field stone; and green for the plant matter and landscapes that remain part of Andrew’s intellectual history.
