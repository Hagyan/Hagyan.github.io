About

A little orientation.

A compact account of where I am, what I study, and the interests that organize this site.

I study philosophy and mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania. My academic interests include mathematical logic, finite model theory, and the philosophy of mathematics.

I have also worked in paleobotany and planetary-analog field research. Outside the university, I work in funeral service in the Philadelphia area. These are not separate lives so much as different ways of attending: to structures, records, landscapes, and persons.

This site is meant to be neither a résumé in disguise nor a personal feed. It is a place to make research and thought available in a form that can remain useful.
