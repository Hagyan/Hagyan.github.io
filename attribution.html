On the shape and colors of this site.

Design choices should have a provenance, even when the provenance is simply a story one wishes to keep visible.

This site takes its calm, section-forward architecture from the academic personal sites of Aaron Anderson and Hermann W. Pfefferkorn, alongside the first version of Andrew’s own site. Pfefferkorn’s On Science page suggested making material choices visible rather than treating them as background.

The palette has its own small genealogy: blue-gray for slate and water; iron red for brick, clay, and Philadelphia; olive gray for field stone; and green for the plant matter and landscapes that remain part of Andrew’s intellectual history.
