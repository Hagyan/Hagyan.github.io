# MAIS-O11 Part 1: adversarial audit and a candidate mechanism

This note concerns the shortest-proof inequality. It makes no claim of a Part 1 witness for ordinary PA-bin.

## The existing padded system: valid only under a weak reading

The construction in `MAISO11SharedConversePass/CONTINUATION-NOTE.md` is coherent under the agenda's three listed requirements for an efficient system. It adds sound shortcut axioms `A -> Q_i`, where `Q_i = ¬¬(i=i)`, and requires a quadratic literal trailer on proof files concluding with negation. Thus the direct proof length `d_i` is at least `(|Q_i|+1)^2`, whereas a proof of `Box_S Q_i -> Q_i` is one shortcut axiom of affine written length. The elementary asymptotic inequality is verified in `PadBounds.lean`. This is a metamathematical construction; the full proof predicate, quotation length and soundness have not been formalized in Lean.

The agenda also asserts that implications compose at a *constant* additive overhead. The padded system fails this property. The fixed short theorem `U` together with the one-line special axiom `U -> Q_i` would produce a linear-size proof of `Q_i` if constant-cost MP composition held, contradicting the quadratic terminal fee. This is an exact obstruction independent of any conjecture about PA. In fact the general principle is: whenever `ℓ_S(U)` is bounded and `ℓ_S(U -> Q_i)` is at most `a|Q_i|+b`, constant-cost MP forces `ℓ_S(Q_i) ≤ a|Q_i|+b+ℓ_S(U)+c_S`. Any proposed direct lower bound above that threshold is impossible. `CompositionBarrier.lean` proves the numerical instance. The weak three-condition reading and the agenda's claimed composition property must be distinguished in any answer advertised as Part 1.

## Why finite Gödel diagonalization does not immediately finish the intended version

A tempting candidate is a short fixed point `G_N` for `¬Bew_{S,≤N}(⌜G_N⌝)`, with `N` written in binary. Assuming the usual formalized proof-representation property and consistency of `S`, there is no `S` proof of `G_N` with written size at most `N`: a purported short proof yields an `S` proof of the bounded proof predicate, while the fixed point yields its negation. If the fixed point's written size and a proof of `Box_S G_N -> G_N` were both polynomial in `log N`, this would give an enormous candidate Part 1 separation (and refute the proposed polynomial Part 2 bound for that system).

The **exact missing step** is the short reflection proof. The bounded fixed point only supplies `G_N -> ¬Bew_{S,≤N}(G_N)`; it does not supply `Bew_S(G_N) -> G_N`. An unbounded proof of `G_N` need not have size at most `N`. More fundamentally, by Löb's theorem an `S` proof of `Bew_S(G_N) -> G_N` yields an `S` proof of `G_N`; the candidate's lower bound then constrains the formalized Löb conversion itself. We cannot infer the short reflection proof from the bounded diagonal lemma, and treating the former as automatic is a circular use of the statement under investigation.

A special axiom `Box_S G_N -> G_N` for a selected family could supply that missing reflection cheaply, but proving the resulting self-referential axiom system consistent/conservative and proving *all* direct proofs of `G_N` are long are then the hard parts. A proof of `Box_S G_N` might combine with the special axiom by constant-cost MP. Assuming merely that each `G_N` is true does not establish lower bounds in the strengthened system. The earlier artificial trailer bypasses the lower-bound problem precisely by violating constant-cost implication composition.

More sharply, let `F_S(r,n)` be *any* valid upper bound for converting a reflection proof of length at most `r`, for a sentence of size `n`, into a direct `S` proof. For a consistent `S` and the bounded fixed points above, necessarily `N < F_S(ℓ_S(Box_S G_N -> G_N), |G_N|)`. Thus a purported `O(poly(log N))` reflection proof in a system also enjoying polynomial `F_S` would contradict consistency for sufficiently large `N`. This conditional trade-off is immediate from the lower bound and the definition of `F_S`; it cannot establish either the existence of short reflection proofs or polynomial `F_S` on its own. It also explains why adding all those reflection formulas as special axioms threatens consistency if a sufficiently cheap Löb conversion is simultaneously available.

## An adversarial test for future candidates

For every proposed family `P_i`, identify the exact system and its constant MP cost `c_S`, if claimed. List every short axiom or derived implication `A -> P_i`; if `A` has a short proof, immediately upper-bound `ℓ_S(P_i)` by `ℓ_S(A)+ℓ_S(A -> P_i)+c_S`. If the only short implication is `Box_S P_i -> P_i`, identify a *uniform* lower-bound proof excluding short `Box_S P_i` and all alternate proofs of `P_i`; testing an exhibited proof alone is insufficient. For bounded Gödel candidates specifically, separate the external no-short-direct-proof argument from an actual short object-level proof of unbounded reflection. Enderton's full axiom schemes, generalization, abbreviation syntax and proof-file checker must be included when the target is PA-bin.

This audit leaves a concrete research route: seek a self-referential, **conservative** extension with cheaply provable selected reflection axioms and an unconditional lower-bound invariant stable under constant-cost MP. No such invariant or complete Part 1 witness is established here.
